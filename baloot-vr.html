Baloot VR — بلوت

The beloved Saudi Baloot card game, played around a virtual table in VR.

Baloot VR brings the classic four-player Baloot card game to Meta Quest: sit at a sunset majlis table with three AI opponents, bid الذوب / الطلب / الرفع (Hukm, Sun, Pass), and play full tricks with real Baloot rules — entirely offline, with natural hand-tracking pinch controls, controller support, and gaze-dwell fallback.

  • Fully offline single-player — no accounts, no data collection, no ads
  • Hand tracking, controllers, and gaze input supported
  • Arabic-first interface with English subtitles

Status: In review on the Meta Horizon Store.

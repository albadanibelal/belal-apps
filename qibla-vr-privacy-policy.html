Privacy Policy — Qibla VR

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Overview

Qibla VR is a native Meta Quest app that shows the direction of the Qibla (the direction toward Makkah) in virtual reality.

Location data

  • The app uses your selected location only to calculate the Qibla bearing (the direction from your position to Makkah) and the distance to Makkah.
  • Your location is stored only on your device (in the app's on-device storage) so the app remembers your city between sessions.
  • Your location is never transmitted to any server, analytics service, advertiser, or third party. The app has no backend, no account system, and sends no personal data over the network.

Other data

  • The app does not collect, store, or share personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, contacts, or files.
  • The app does not access the camera or microphone.
  • To render your hands and detect pinch gestures when you use the app without controllers, the app reads hand-tracking data (hand and finger positions) from the headset. This data is processed only on your device in real time to show your hands and recognize gestures; it is never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

Children's privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will replace this document.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Meta Horizon Store listing contact channel.
